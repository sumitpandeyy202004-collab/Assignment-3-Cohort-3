SHINSEI VILLAGE

Genuine storytelling project

VISION

Shinsei Village is a story-driven world where ancient legends, mysterious warriors, and powerful spirits come together. At the heart of this journey are the demigod monkeys who inhabit a hidden village surrounded by mountains, temples, and forgotten secrets.
Our vision is to build more than a collection. We want to create an evolving universe where the community becomes part of the story, discovers its mysteries, and helps shape the future of Shinsei Village through adventures, challenges, and shared experiences.
